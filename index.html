Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). It brings growers, processors, roasters, buyers and policymakers together for three days of trade, learning and tasting at Speke Resort Munyonyo.

Visitors can meet exhibitors from across the coffee value chain, attend sessions led by regional and international speakers, and explore new markets for Ugandan coffee. Whether you grow it, sell it or simply love it, there is something here for you.
